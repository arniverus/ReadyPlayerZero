READY PLAYER ZERO

An interactive literary-analysis quest through Ready Player One

Guiding Question: How does the author strategically employ literary elements, narrative choices, and perspective to construct meaning, and how does examining the text through different literary lenses complicate or deepen that meaning?

1 · Solve the riddle. Each riddle hints at an object hidden in the world. Find it and click it.
2 · Choose a claim. You'll see competing interpretations. Pick ONE you can defend.
3 · Prove it. Select the 2 of 6 text-evidence cards that logically support your claim.
4 · Defend & reconsider. Explain the author's move, then answer a competing view.
